## Overview

Tinku is a corporate benefits and wellness platform designed to help companies manage health, insurance, and employee support services from a unified digital experience.

## Key Features

- **Corporate Benefits Management**: Centralized flows for wellness, health, and protection services.
- **Responsive Web Experience**: Interfaces adapted for users reviewing plans, benefits, and service information.
- **Operational Support**: Structure prepared for quote requests, service visibility, and business follow-up.
- **Deployment Ready**: Published as a production web platform with a public client-facing URL.

## Technologies Used

- **React / Next.js**
- **TypeScript**
- **Responsive UI**
- **Cloud Deployment**

## Challenges and Learnings

The main challenge was organizing a service-heavy experience into a clear, trustworthy interface that communicates benefits quickly while keeping the platform maintainable and scalable.

## Outcome

Tinku became a digital platform for presenting and managing corporate wellness and protection services, supporting business users with a polished and accessible web experience.
